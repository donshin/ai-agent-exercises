## Scope summary

- 220 Cat6A drops total (approx. 112 on floor 2, 108 on floor 3), incl. AP, printer and wall-mount locations
- 8 PoE switches (48-port), rack-mounted in TR-2 and TR-3, 4 per floor
- Patch panels, cable management, fiber riser tie-in between floors
- Labeling to Larkfield's scheme (floor-room-port, e.g. R2-TRA-014)
- Certification testing on every drop, results delivered as PDF report
- As-built drawings at closeout

## Site assumptions

- Building mgmt provides riser + ceiling access, and freight elevator for material deliveries
- Drop-tile ceilings throughout (hard-lid area on floor 3 east side still to verify)
- Dedicated 20A circuit in each TR, installed by building electrician before we arrive
- Racks in place (Larkfield/Facilities)
- Furniture not yet installed on either floor during the pull

## Crew

- 1 lead tech + 5 installers on site
- 2 certification techs join on the last day
